# Getting started

kb is a knowledge base for your own documents. You upload files, kb splits them into passages, and you can then search them or ask questions that are answered from them, with sources.

## Find your way around

The sidebar on the left leads to every area:

- **Chat**: ask questions about your documents. See [Chat](chat.md).
- **Documents**: everything in your knowledge base. See [Documents](documents.md).
- **Search**: find passages across all documents. See [Search](search.md).
- **Ingestion**: upload files to add them. See [Ingestion](ingestion.md).
- **Models**: check that the language model behind search and chat is available. See [Models](models.md).
- **Help**, at the bottom of the sidebar: this manual, one page per area.

**kb / Knowledge Base** at the top of the sidebar takes you to the home page. Type a question into its box and press Enter to start a new chat with it. Below the box are shortcuts to **Search documents** and **Upload documents**, and cards that lead to chatting, searching, uploading and the models.

## Add your first documents

1. Open **Ingestion**.
2. Drop files onto the upload area, or select **Choose files**. PDF, Markdown and plain text files work.
3. Wait until each file shows **Done** under **Uploads**.

The files then appear under **Documents** with the status **Ready**. More in [Upload documents](ingestion.md#upload-documents).

## Ask your first question

1. Open **Chat** and select **New chat**.
2. Type your question in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
3. The answer appears as it is written. Numbers like [1] point to the passages it is based on; the sources are listed next to the answer.

More in [Ask a question](chat.md#ask-a-question).

## Switch between light and dark mode

The switch at the bottom of the sidebar has three options: **System** (follow your computer's setting), **Light** and **Dark**. kb remembers your choice in this browser.
