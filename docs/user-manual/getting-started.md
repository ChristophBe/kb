# Getting started

Knowcelium is where you keep your own documents and ask questions about them. You upload files, Knowcelium splits them into passages, and you can then search them or ask questions that are answered from them, with sources.

## Sign in

Knowcelium asks you to sign in when you open it, and shows who is signed in at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Sign in](sign-in.md).

## Find your way around

The sidebar on the left leads to every area:

- **Chat**: ask questions about your documents. See [Chat](chat.md).
- **Documents**: everything in your account. See [Documents](documents.md).
- **Upload**: add files. See [Upload](ingestion.md).
- **Teams**: create teams and manage who is in them. See [Teams](teams.md).
- **Users**, for platform admins only: see who has signed in and who is a platform admin. See [Users](users.md).
- **Models**: check that the language model behind search and chat is available. See [Models](models.md).
- **Help**, at the bottom of the sidebar: this manual, one page per area.

The search box at the top of the sidebar searches from anywhere; press ⌘K or Ctrl+K to jump into it. See [Search from any page](search.md#search-from-any-page).

Knowcelium opens on **Chat**.

When you open an area, your keyboard focus moves to the page's title, so you can start reading or press Tab to reach its first control without going back through the sidebar. To jump past the sidebar at any time, press Tab right after the page loads: the first stop is **Skip to content**, and Enter takes you to the page itself.

If you type an address that doesn't exist, you see **Page not found** with a link, **Go to the start page**.

## Add your first documents

1. Open **Upload**.
2. Drop files onto the upload area, or select **Choose files**. PDF, Markdown and plain text files work.
3. Wait until each file shows **Done** under **Uploads**.

The files then appear under **Documents** with the status **Ready**. More in [Upload documents](ingestion.md#upload-documents).

## Ask your first question

1. Open **Chat** and select **New chat**. On an empty workspace, Chat points you to the upload page first.
2. Type your question in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
3. The answer appears as it is written. Numbers like [1] point to the passages it is based on; the sources are listed next to the answer.

More in [Ask a question](chat.md#ask-a-question).

## Switch between light and dark mode

The switch at the bottom of the sidebar has three options: **System** (follow your computer's setting), **Light** and **Dark**. Knowcelium remembers your choice in this browser.

## When a page can't be loaded

Knowcelium loads each area the first time you open it. If that fails, the page says, for example, **Chat couldn't be loaded.** This usually means Knowcelium was updated while you had it open. Select **Reload page** to get the current version; the rest of Knowcelium keeps working in the meantime.
