# Sign in

kb shows your documents and conversations only after you sign in, so it knows who you are. You sign in on your organisation's sign-in page, and kb then brings you back to where you were going.

## Sign in to kb

1. Open kb, or a link to any page in it.
2. If you aren't signed in, kb sends you to the sign-in page. Enter your email address and password there and confirm.
3. kb opens the page you asked for.

While kb gets you signed in it shows **Signing in…**. If the sign-in page can't be reached, kb shows **Couldn't sign you in** with **Sign-in isn't available right now. Try again in a moment.** Select **Try again** once it's back. If the sign-in page turned you away, kb says why; select **Try again** to start over.

## See who is signed in

The bottom of the sidebar shows the name and email address you are signed in with.

## Stay signed in

kb keeps you signed in in this browser, across tabs and when you reload a page, and renews your sign-in in the background while you work. When your sign-in can't be renewed any more, kb shows **Your session has expired. Signing in again…** and sends you to the sign-in page; after signing in you come back to the page you were on. Text you had typed but not sent yet, such as an unsent chat question, is lost.

## Sign out

1. Select **Sign out**, the button next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. kb shows **You're signed out**. Select **Sign in** to sign in again, for example as someone else.

Signing out ends your session in this browser.
