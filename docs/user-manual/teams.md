# Teams

Teams group people who work together. You can belong to several teams, create your own and see their members on the Teams page. Teams are what you [share documents](sharing-documents.md) with: the members of a team can read the documents shared with it.

## Create a team

1. Open **Teams** in the sidebar.
2. Type a name under **New team** and select **Create team**.

Knowcelium opens the new team. You are its first admin.

## See your teams and their members

**Teams** lists the teams you belong to. An **Admin** badge marks the ones you administer. Select a team to see its members with their email address and role:

- **Admin**: can rename and delete the team and manage its members and roles.
- **Member**: can see the team and its members and can leave it.

You only see teams you belong to. If you open a team you aren't in, Knowcelium says it doesn't exist, or you aren't a member.

## Add a member

Only admins can add members.

1. Open the team.
2. Under **Add a member by email**, type the person's email address and select **Add member**.

The person joins as a **Member**. They must have signed in to Knowcelium at least once; otherwise Knowcelium says "No one with this email address is registered yet", and you can try again after they have signed in. Adding someone who already belongs to the team shows "This person is already a member."

## Change a member's role

Admins choose **Admin** or **Member** in the **Role** list next to a member. A team always keeps at least one admin: if you try to make the last admin a member, Knowcelium says "A team needs at least one admin. Make someone else an admin first." Every change shows a short confirmation. If you make yourself a member, Knowcelium asks you to confirm first, because only another admin can make you an admin again.

## Remove a member

Admins select the remove button next to a member (**Remove** and their name) and confirm. The same rule applies: the last admin can't be removed.

## Leave a team

1. Open the team and select **Leave team** in your own row.
2. Confirm with **Leave**.

The last admin can't leave: make someone else an admin first, or delete the team.

## Rename or delete a team

Admins select **Rename** to change the name. **Delete team** removes the team and its memberships after you confirm; this can't be undone. Documents that were shared with the team stop being visible to its former members, and are private to their owners again unless they are shared with other teams.

## What a team gives its members

Belonging to a team lets you read the documents that are shared with it, find them in search and use them in chat. It doesn't let you change them, and it shows nobody anything else of yours. Being an admin of a team only adds managing the team and its members, and [stopping sharing](sharing-documents.md#stop-sharing-a-document-with-your-team) a document with your team. Platform admins, who [designate other platform admins](users.md), get no extra access to documents either.
